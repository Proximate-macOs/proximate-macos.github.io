Proximate

Proximate brings time-sensitive information to where your attention lives: right next to your mouse cursor. Notifications, app badges, and the clock follow your pointer—eliminating glances to distant screen edges.

On large displays, critical updates hide in corners outside your central vision. Proximate fixes this with cursor-anchored icons for selected apps and a persistent clock, all fully customizable.

Cursor-Anchored Notifications

Monitor apps you choose. Their icons appear near your pointer only when unread notifications arrive. Switch to the app, and they vanish—keeping your workflow uninterrupted.

Cursor Clock

A compact, always-visible clock trails your mouse. No more menu bar glances. Choose 12h or 24h format, adjust size, opacity, and distance for seamless integration.

Precise Customization

Tailor icon size, offset from cursor, transparency, and positioning. Fine-tune to match your setup, from ultrawide monitors to compact laptops.

Global Hotkey Control

Instantly toggle all elements with a keyboard shortcut. Show or hide Proximate across apps without disrupting your flow.

Non-Intrusive Design

Enhances macOS notifications without replacing them. Accessibility-focused for low peripheral vision—information stays where you look.

How It Works

1. Select Apps

Choose which apps to monitor for notifications and badges in Proximate settings.

2. Position Elements

Customize icon size, clock format, distance, and opacity to fit your preferences.

3. Move Your Cursor

Icons and clock follow automatically. They appear only when needed, disappear on app switch.

4. Toggle Anytime

Use the global hotkey to show or hide everything instantly.

FAQ

Does it replace macOS notifications?

No. Proximate adds a cursor-adjacent layer for faster awareness, complementing the system.

Is it safe and lightweight?

Yes. Native macOS utility with minimal CPU usage, respecting your system's performance.

Can I customize everything?

Fully. Icon size, clock style, opacity, distance, and hotkey—all adjustable.

What about accessibility?

Designed for it. Helps users with peripheral vision challenges by centralizing info.
